About

I am a PhD candidate in the national PhD program in Robotics and Intelligent Machines, run with the University of Genova, and I work in the Humanoid Sensing and Perception group at the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia. My research is on controlling anthropomorphic hands equipped with tactile sensors, with a focus on collaborative tasks such as robot-to-human handover.

Before the PhD I was a research fellow in the same group, writing the hand controllers and grasp planners for the ergoCub humanoid and characterising its tactile sensors. Earlier I worked in automotive software at Teoresi, and did my master's thesis at the ICAROS Center in Naples on model predictive control for autonomous suturing with the da Vinci Research Kit.

Force-aware graspingMulti-fingered control that regulates contact forces on humanoid hands.
Tactile handoverTemporal touch encoding and compliance to read human intent during bimanual handover.
Touch + visionProprioception and multi-contact touch for 3D reconstruction under hand occlusion.
